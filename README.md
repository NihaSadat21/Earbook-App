# 🎧 EarBook

**Your bookshelf, now with a voice.**

EarBook brings ebooks to your ears. Snap a photo or scan any printed page and hear it
read aloud, or explore a free library of open books ready to play. No subscriptions,
no paywalls, no cost. Just press play and listen.

## ✨ Highlights
- 💸 **100% free:** no sign-ups to pay for, no subscriptions, no hidden costs
- 📷 **Page to audio:** turn a photo or scanned page into speech in seconds
- 📚 **Free audio library:** hundreds of public-domain and Creative Commons books
- ♿ **Accessible by design:** built so everyone can enjoy reading
- 🌍 **Multilingual:** listen in multiple languages

## 🛠️ Built With
Python · Flask · OCR · Text-to-Speech
